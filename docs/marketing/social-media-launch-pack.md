South Africa has a problem with handymen.

Not with the workers — most of them are skilled, hardworking, and just need consistent opportunity. The problem is the system around them. Or the lack of one.

We built something about this. Dickson is South Africa's handyman platform — built properly, from the ground up.

- We provide transport (Dickson Van) for workers who need it
- Purpose-built safety features, especially for women living alone

This isn't just a marketplace. It's a system that creates dignified, consistent work for South African tradespeople while giving homeowners peace of mind.
